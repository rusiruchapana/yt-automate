    Your YouTube channel

    Local rendering does not need a Google account.

    1. Enable YouTube Data API v3 in your Google project.
    2. Create a Desktop app OAuth client and download its JSON.
    3. Save it as data/secrets/client_secrets.json.
    4. From the project folder, run:
      docker compose run --rm --service-ports auth
    5. Open the printed Google URL on this computer and choose your channel.

    The full guide covers test users, Brand Accounts, token expiry and reconnecting. Never share your data/secrets folder.

    Queue settings

    A channel-specific YouTube limit can be lower than your configured cap. Keep your laptop awake until uploads finish. Already-uploaded schedules work while it is off.

    YOUR FIRST SESSION

    From zero to a local preview.

    Everything needed to render is free and runs on your computer.

    01

    Try the demo

    Click “Try the free demo.” It creates original abstract backgrounds, an original tone loop and a 12-second quiz. Wait for the preview. Demo jobs cannot be uploaded.

    02

    Add your own collection

    Use Media & music. Add your own or cleared visuals and tracks. Record permission and exact required credit. Use Edit to choose a starting section of a track or clip.

    03

    Write a distinct video

    Use 3–6 short scene paragraphs. Choose an audience based on the actual content. Give facts and quizzes a source. Preview the rendered video and approve the finished result.

    04

    Connect and test privately

    Follow YouTube & settings and the full PDF guide. Connect through Google's official page. Verify the channel name. Enable uploads with a cap of 1 for your first private test.

    05

    Prepare hourly slots

    Fill the CSV with different, finished scripts. Set delivery to scheduled and choose a future batch start. Use 60-minute spacing. Set your rolling cap up to 24 if your channel allows it.

    06

    Keep it running

    Keep the app and laptop awake while producing and uploading. The queue stops at its cap and holds missed slots. Back up your data folder. This free edition does not generate AI footage, scrape clips or create new scripts unattended.

    Useful commands

    docker compose up -d --build
docker compose logs --tail 80 studio
docker compose stop
docker compose start

    Read GUIDE.md or the included PDF for Windows setup, native Linux setup, script CSV examples, backup commands and recovery steps.
